# Privacy policy

The extension stores what you enter in its settings - the address of your Kimai
server and your personal API token - in the browser's own extension storage
(`chrome.storage.sync`). It also remembers the project and type of work you last
picked, so the next entry is quicker to start.

The extension talks to one host only: the Kimai server whose address you entered.
Nothing is sent anywhere else. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking,
no advertising identifier, and no third-party service of any kind.

The authors do not receive, see, or store any of your data. All communication is
between your browser and your own Kimai instance.

Removing the extension removes everything it stored.
